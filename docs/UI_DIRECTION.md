# Menu direction

Visual thesis: a quiet deep-green surface and birch-gold action sit over the actual forest scene, keeping the hero leaf and its setting central.

Content plan: the title and Begin journey lead; How to play, Settings and Credits supply details. Pause offers resume/restart/home. Damage uses James's exact ending text and two recovery options. The preview label describes the currently implemented headwaters reach.

Interaction thesis: a short opacity entrance, a restrained focus/hover shift and the existing physical canopy descent connect menus to play. Reduced-motion preferences remove menu animation. Native dialog, visible focus and keyboard/controller navigation retain a single clear active surface. Gameplay and ambient audio pause while menus are open; settings do not simulate elapsed menu time.

No framework, image assets, music or new gameplay mechanics are added for the interface. Audio effects are original Web Audio synthesis; their feel and menu art remain subject to James's acceptance.
